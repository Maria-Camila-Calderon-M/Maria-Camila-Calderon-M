## 🌱 About me

I am an **economist trained to measure what matters**. My quantitative toolkit includes **econometrics, environmental impact assessment and development economics**, which I use to assess development outcomes, evaluate **ESG risks** and structure evidence for **investment and policy decisions** in **R** and **Stata**.

I have applied these skills across **government agencies in three countries** (Colombia, the United States and France), with a focus on **sustainable industries, inclusive trade and the economic empowerment of underserved communities**.

My current **Master's in Innovation, Human Development & Sustainability (IHDS)** at the **Université de Genève** is deepening my understanding of **impact finance, climate governance and the SDG frameworks** that shape how public and private capital can be deployed more effectively.

I work best **at the intersection of data and decision-making**, where rigorous analysis meets the complexity of real-world development challenges. Let's work together to drive transformative change! ✨
